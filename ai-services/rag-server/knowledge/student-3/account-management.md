# KICKLAB Customer Account Management

The Customer Account Management feature is owned by Student 3.

Customers can view their account summary, including their first name, last name,
email address, phone number, account status, and account creation date.

Customers can edit their first name, last name, email address, and phone number
from the Account Summary page.

Customers can also change their password and delete their account.

The Student 3 backend runs on port 5003 and the customer database API runs on
port 6003.

The standard customer API does not expose password hashes in normal customer
responses.
